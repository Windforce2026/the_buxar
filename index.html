About Buxar

A City Where History Meets the Future

Buxar, carved on the sacred Ganga, is a land of ancient heritage, deep spirituality, vibrant culture, and natural beauty. Quiet lanes, historic temples and a rising commercial ambition all pulse with one rhythm — honouring a proud past while stepping boldly into tomorrow.
