Trust & Transparency

Policies & Information

How this website handles privacy, listings, payments, corrections and advertising — written to describe what the site actually does today.

Last updated · 1 November 2026

TheBuxar.com is a district information website for Buxar, Bihar. It is built as a static site: the pages you read are files, not a logged-in service. This page explains the limited data the site keeps, how listings and marketplace records work, and how to ask for a correction or an advertising placement.

Privacy

The site has no server-side account system, no visitor database and no login that stores your details on a server. Reading pages, browsing the directory or searching does not create a profile with us.

The contact form on the Contact page does not submit to a server. It prepares a message and opens WhatsApp, where WhatsApp's own privacy terms apply. The footer email form is not connected to a mailing service yet, so it does not save your address.

If you email us or message us on WhatsApp, we receive what you choose to send — typically your name, contact details and message — and use it only to reply or to act on your request. There is no newsletter list or marketing database behind this site.

The hosting provider may keep standard technical logs, such as an IP address and the page requested, for security and reliability. The site itself does not install third-party analytics, advertising or tracking scripts.

Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives the request needed to serve the font files. Links to WhatsApp, email and any external source take you to services with their own policies.

Cookies & browser storage

The site does not set cookies. It uses your browser's local storage to remember a few settings, so the site feels the same when you return:

  • Theme and language preference.
  • The district area you last selected.
  • Account, saved-business, claimed-profile and community drafts stored by the site's on-device tools.
  • A session flag so the intro animation is not replayed on every page.

This information stays in your browser. It is not uploaded to us. Clearing site data in your browser removes it, and on-device accounts or drafts cannot be recovered afterwards.

Business listings

Directory records describe businesses and services in Buxar district. A record may be supplied by the business, claimed from an unclaimed entry, or compiled from public information. The Verified badge is shown only where a record is marked verified in the directory data; it is not a guarantee of quality, price or current opening hours.

Some fields are left blank when we do not have a checked value. Phone numbers, addresses, hours and categories should be confirmed directly with the business before you rely on them.

If you own a listed business, you can use the claim option on its profile to correct the details. If a record is wrong, unfair or should be removed, write to us with the profile link and the change you are asking for.

Marketplace & payments

The marketplace introduces buyers to local sellers and makers. TheBuxar.com does not process payments, hold money, or take a commission on an order. Payment, delivery and returns are settled directly between the buyer and the seller, usually by UPI, cash or direct transfer.

Prices, stock, delivery areas and return terms are set by the seller. Check them with the seller before paying. A sample record used to demonstrate the layout is marked as demo in the site data and is not an offer to sell.

Never send bank details, one-time passwords or card numbers to a seller you have not verified. If a marketplace conversation feels wrong, stop and report it to us.

News, events & corrections

Current Buxar publishes a story only after it has been checked against a named source. Where no verified story exists, the page shows an empty state instead of a placeholder article. Demo records in the site data are never presented as current news.

Event dates, venues and timings can change at short notice. Always confirm with the organiser before travelling. A listing on this site is information, not an endorsement of the event.

To request a correction, email hello@thebuxar.com with the page URL, what is wrong and a source we can check. We aim to correct verified factual errors promptly and will say so on the page when a correction is material.

Advertising & sponsorship

Paid placements, sponsored listings and promoted business profiles are labelled as such. Advertising does not buy editorial coverage, a news story, a review score or a Verified badge. News and corrections remain independent of any advertiser.

To ask about advertising, email hello@thebuxar.com or message +91 98180 48920 on WhatsApp with the subject “Advertising”. Tell us what you want to promote, where in the district it operates, and the dates you have in mind. Nothing is published and no payment is taken on this website until the placement and its label are agreed in writing.

The Deals section stays marked “soon” until real, verified offers exist. We do not invent discounts, prices or partner names to fill a page.

Advertising enquiries are handled by email or WhatsApp. This site has no online booking or payment flow for ads.

Terms of use

This website is provided for general information about Buxar district. We work to keep it accurate, but listings, prices, timings, routes and events change. The site is provided as it is, without a warranty that every entry is complete or current.

External links lead to services we do not control. Their content, availability and privacy practices are their own. Images and archival plates are used under the licences listed on the Image Credits page; names and marks belong to their respective owners.

Please do not scrape the site at scale, impersonate a business you do not own, submit false listings, or use the contact channels to harass anyone. We may remove a listing or restrict a tool that is being misused.

We may update these policies as the site changes. The date at the top of this page shows the current version.
