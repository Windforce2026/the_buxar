Explore Buxar

The Whole District, Mapped

This is the district as a guidebook — two towns and four rural areas, each with its own page. Buxar Town is the seat of the district, Dumraon the old capital of a Raj, Brahampur the country of Baba Brahmeshwar Nath, Chausa the field of 1539 and the name on a mango, and Itarhi with Rajpur the wide agricultural plains. What is genuinely known from published sources is stated; what could not be confirmed is left out.

Sources & honesty. All six destinations are documented from verified sources — the district administration, the Census of 2011, railway and tourist records. Coordinates are shown only where they are published and confirmed; nothing on the map is estimated. Verify routes, timings and fees locally before travelling.
