Technical note
--------------
Placeholder files are JPEG-encoded image data saved under a .webp
extension. Browsers render them correctly (format sniffing); they are
named .webp so the client's professional photography can drop in later
with the same filename. Replace, do not compress further.

Every image used on the tourism page either has an on-page caption with
its credit or is clearly marked "photography pending". No image is
passed off as a photograph of a specific monument unless it is one.

Fair-licensing policy
---------------------
No image was pulled directly from a tourism listing or news site
without a free license. The "Baba Brahmeshwar Nath Temple" reference
image that circulated online was NOT redistributed: its usage rights
could not be confirmed, so a clearly-marked placeholder stands in its
place until the client provides their own photography.
