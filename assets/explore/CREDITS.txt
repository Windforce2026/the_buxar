TheBuxar.com — Explore Buxar section image credits
====================================================
Each folder under assets/explore/<slug>/ holds the hero image for one of
the six featured places. No image is passed off as a photograph of a
specific monument unless it is one; otherwise the credit line says
"representative".

buxar-town/hero.webp
    Ganga riverfront at Buxar at sunset. TheBuxar.com site asset
    (hero-buxar-sunset.jpg, via assets/tourism/spiritual/ganga-sunrise.webp)
    — client photography.

dumraon/hero.webp
    "Buxar on the Buxar, Bihar" — drawn by Robert Smith, painted
    1828-1833, published 1844 (V&A collection). Public domain.
    Used here as a representative heritage view of the riverside town;
    it is an 1828 town view, not a photograph of Dumraon itself.

brahampur/hero.webp
    Placeholder file (awaiting client photography), reused from
    assets/tourism/spiritual/brahmeshwar-nath.webp. Representative image
    for the Shiva-temple country of Brahampur.

chausa/hero.webp
    Diagram of the Battle of Chausa (1539) battle formation, redrawn by
    Shakti (Wikimedia Commons), CC BY-SA 4.0 (or PD). Reused from
    assets/tourism/historical/chausa.webp.

itarhi/hero.webp
    Paddy fields near Buxar — Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA (shared source
    with assets/history/paddy-fields-buxar.jpg and
    assets/tourism/historical/paddy-fields.webp).

rajpur/hero.jpg
